What the apps can and cannot do

Measured against Jacobson's principles for elementary piano
Can assess
  • ✓Note reading and rhythm
  • ✓Error correction
  • ✓Slow, sectioned practice
Cannot assess
  • ✕Phrasing and dynamics
  • ✕Note duration and sustain
  • ✕Tone production
  • ✕Fingering and coordination

05 · Hybrid model

A 6-step hybrid sequence

At home, with the app

1Note learning

Pitch only, before any rhythm

2Score-singing

Sing alone, then with the demo

3Add rhythm

Slow and steady, then gradually faster

4Smoothness

Remove pauses, as the style allows

In the lesson, with the teacher

5Teacher time

Fingering, wrist, articulation, dynamics, interpretation

6Practice calendar

Dates, duration and pieces practiced: teacher and app
